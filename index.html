How it works

  1. Enable the microphone and play your instrument — the tuner shows the note it hears, live.
  2. Arm a track (⏺ on a track header), pick Notes or Audio mode.
  3. Hit Record in the transport and play along. Notes mode transcribes your playing into editable notes; Audio mode records the sound itself.
  4. Stop — your take is in the song. Open the clip to fix any note.

🧩 Loop & Pattern Library

Click a loop to drop it at the playhead. Drum loops go to a drum track, everything else gets its own instrument track.

BeatForge Studio — Help

Making music

  • Add tracks with + Instrument, + Drums, + Audio.
  • Double-click an empty lane to create a clip; double-click a clip to edit it (piano roll or drum grid).
  • Drag clips to move, drag their right edge to resize, Ctrl+D duplicates, Delete removes.
  • Drag on the timeline ruler to set a loop region; click it to move the playhead.
  • Use 🧩 Loops for ready-made beats, basslines, chords and melodies.
  • Mix with each track's volume, pan, reverb and delay sends; Mute / Solo per track.
  • ⬇ WAV mixes your whole song down to an audio file.

Recording your instrument

  • Open 🎤 Mic / Tuner, enable the mic, and play — the note you're playing shows live.
  • Arm a track, press Record, play along with the click. In Notes mode your playing is transcribed into notes you can edit; in Audio mode the raw sound is recorded.
  • The 🎹 Keys panel records the same way — play the virtual keyboard while recording.

Shortcuts

  • Space play/pause · R record · M metronome · L loop
  • Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo/redo · Ctrl+D duplicate clip
  • A–P row plays notes · Z / X octave down/up
